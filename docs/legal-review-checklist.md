# Legal review checklist

_Generated by `npm run legal:checklist` from `src/messages/*.json`. Do not edit by hand._

The privacy policy (`/privacy`) and terms (`/terms`) are **drafts**. Every passage that makes a legal claim or needs the operator's own
details starts with `[NEEDS LEGAL REVIEW]` and is shown highlighted on the page. After a lawyer has checked a passage, edit its text in all three
language files and delete the mark there. The draft banner disappears by itself when no mark is left.

**Open marks: 14.** Run `npm run legal:checklist -- --check` before launch: it fails while any mark remains.

Also fill in (these are placeholders on the pages until set):

* `NEXT_PUBLIC_OPERATOR_NAME` and `NEXT_PUBLIC_OPERATOR_COUNTRY`: who runs the service.
* `NEXT_PUBLIC_CONTACT_EMAIL`: shown on both pages.
* The date line ("Draft of ...") in `legal.privacy.updated` and `legal.terms.updated`.
